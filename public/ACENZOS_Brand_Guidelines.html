03 — Brand Story
Born from an unwavering belief
that excellence is never accidental.

ACENZOS was conceived at the intersection of intention and craft. Every curve in our mark, every weight in our typeface, every tone in our palette was deliberated — not chosen by chance, but by conviction.

The name itself carries resonance — an accent, an edge, a sense of ascent. ACENZOS is the mark of those who move forward with grace, whose standards are not aspirational but lived.

04 — Brand Personality
Character & Voice

08 — Brand Mark Misuse
What to Avoid

Protect the integrity of the ACENZOS mark at all times. The following misuses compromise brand equity and must never be employed in any application.

✕
Do Not
Do not stretch, squash, skew or distort the logo mark in any dimension. Proportions are fixed and must be preserved exactly.
✕
Do Not
Do not apply drop shadows, glows, bevels or any filter effects to the logo. The mark stands alone in its simplicity.
✕
Do Not
Do not use the logo in any colour outside the approved palette. Black and white only — no exceptions.
✕
Do Not
Do not rotate or flip the logo. It must always appear in its original upright orientation without exception.
✕
Do Not
Do not place the logo on busy or patterned backgrounds that compromise legibility and visual contrast.
✕
Do Not
Do not reproduce the logo below 20 mm in print or 56 px on screen. Clarity and impact are irretrievably lost at smaller sizes.

12
Brand
Typeface

ACENZOS uses Cormorant Garamond for grandeur and Montserrat for precision — together they cover the full range of the brand voice from editorial headlines to fine body copy.

Under no circumstances should these typefaces be substituted. System fonts and generic sans-serifs are not approved for any brand application.
